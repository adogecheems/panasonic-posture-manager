## 简体中文

**从 3.0.0 起，正式支持 64 位 Windows 10 和 Windows 11。** 松下 Let's note QV8、QV9 和 QV1 用户现在可以安装 Windows 版，翻成帐篷时画面会转正，键盘朝下作为支架时会保持横屏。两种姿态下内置键盘和触控板都保持可用。

- **锁屏与唤醒**：应用已运行时，锁屏会切回自动模式并继续调整内屏方向。QV8、QV9 和 QV1 从睡眠中唤醒后，在稳定的笔记本或帐篷姿态下即可重新识别，无需特意再折叠一次。
- **安装与界面**：Windows 安装向导的完成页可以直接启动应用。应用会记住外观选择；桌面尚在准备时会先显示等待状态，再按需提示重试。
- **下载**：Windows 用户可选简体中文或英文安装程序。Linux 用户可选 Arch Linux、Debian / Ubuntu 或 Fedora 软件包，安装后注销并重新登录。KDE Plasma 6 仍为实验性支持，尚未实机验证。

## English

**Version 3.0.0 marks the start of official support for 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11.** On a Panasonic Let's note QV8, QV9 or QV1, the Windows app turns the picture upright when you fold the laptop into a tent and keeps it in landscape when you rest it keyboard-side down. The built-in keyboard and touchpad stay available in both postures.

- **Lock and resume**: If the app is running when you lock the screen, it switches to automatic mode and continues adjusting the built-in display. After waking a QV8, QV9 or QV1, hold it steady in laptop or tent posture. The app can recognize it again without another fold.
- **Installation and interface**: You can launch the app from the final page of the Windows installer. The app remembers your appearance preference. While the desktop is getting ready, it shows a waiting message before offering a retry if needed.
- **Downloads**: Choose the Simplified Chinese or English installer for Windows. On Linux, choose the Arch Linux, Debian / Ubuntu or Fedora package, then sign out and back in after installation. KDE Plasma 6 support remains experimental and has yet to be verified on hardware.
