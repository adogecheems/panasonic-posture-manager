## 简体中文

3.1.2：修复切回笔记本姿态后的多余屏幕旋转。

- **Linux**：修复笔记本姿态下挂起恢复时，画面可能短暂旋转后再转回的问题。
- **Linux、Windows**：成功切回笔记本方向后，退出程序或恢复桌面时保持正常方向，避免重新转到之前的帐篷或平板方向；原有方向锁偏好仍会恢复。

**升级**：按常规方式升级即可。安装方法与支持范围见[使用说明](../../README.md)。

## English

3.1.2: Fixes unnecessary screen rotation after returning to laptop posture.

- **Linux**: Fixes a brief rotation away and back when resuming from suspend in laptop posture.
- **Linux, Windows**: After successfully returning to laptop orientation, exiting the app or restoring the desktop keeps the normal direction instead of reverting to an earlier tent or tablet direction; the original rotation-lock preference is still restored.

**Upgrading**: Upgrade as usual. See the [user guide](../../README.en.md) for installation and supported systems.
